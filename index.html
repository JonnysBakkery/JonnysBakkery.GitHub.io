Our Story

From one oven to the whole neighbourhood

Jonny started baking in a tiny corner shop with a single wood-fired oven and a sourdough starter he still uses today. What began as bread for friends and neighbours grew into the bakery the whole street now wakes up for.

Today our team of twelve bakers still follows Jonny's simple rule: bake honestly, use the best ingredients, and give every loaf the time it deserves.

  • The same sourdough starter since 1998
  • No preservatives or artificial flavours
  • Leftover bread donated to local food banks every evening
